The TripRide promise

A confirmed journey is a commitment.

A ride can be an important part of someone’s day — getting home, reaching work, meeting family or making a journey that matters. TripRide is designed around responsible trip creation, thoughtful booking and respect for the people depending on that journey.

Quick answers

TripRide, without the fine-print feeling.

What is TripRide?

TripRide is a mobile travel app for discovering trips, sharing rides, managing bookings and connecting around a journey.

What is the TripRide platform fee?

The platform fee shown on this website is ₹50. Applicable transaction details should be displayed in the app before confirmation.

Where do I manage my trips?

The main TripRide experience — including trip and booking tools — is available inside the mobile app.

Does this website show fake user or trip counts?

No. This website does not display invented user counts, trip counts, ratings or testimonials.

What does TripRide mean by committed journeys?

TripRide treats a confirmed trip as a meaningful commitment between people. The experience encourages users to create and book trips thoughtfully because another person may be depending on that journey.
