1. Terms & Conditions

These Terms govern your access to and use of Tripride's website, application and services. By registering, browsing, creating a ride, publishing a listing or making a booking, you agree to these Terms and the applicable policies.

1.1 About Tripride and its role

Tripride is an online technology platform intended to help users discover, publish and connect with other users for trips, ride sharing, place-to-place journeys, daily route sharing, rentals, taxi-related listings, tour packages and vehicle marketplace listings. Unless a specific service page or written agreement expressly states otherwise, Tripride acts as an intermediary that facilitates discovery, communication, booking requests and payment flows between users and independent drivers, publishers, vehicle owners, sellers or service providers.

The person who publishes a ride or listing is referred to as the “Publisher”. A person who requests or makes a booking is referred to as the “Booker” or “Passenger”, as applicable. A user may act in more than one role.

Tripride does not, merely by displaying a listing, become the owner, driver, employer, agent, insurer or operator of a third-party vehicle or transport service. The relevant Publisher remains responsible for the vehicle, driving, permits, insurance, fitness, service delivery, agreed fare and conduct, subject to applicable law. This description of Tripride's role does not exclude any legal responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded or any duty that applies to Tripride as a platform.

1.2 Eligibility and account

  • You must provide accurate, current and complete information and keep your phone number and account details updated.
  • You must be legally capable of entering into a binding contract. Users below 18 years may use the service only where permitted by law and with appropriate parent/guardian involvement; Tripride may restrict accounts for minors.
  • Keep OTPs, passwords, ride codes and TPINs confidential. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account, except where applicable law provides otherwise.
  • Tripride may request identity, driving licence, vehicle, address or other verification information where reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention or legal compliance.

1.3 Accurate listings and bookings

Publishers must accurately describe the route, pickup and drop-off points, date, time, seats, vehicle category, vehicle registration details, fare or cost contribution, luggage limits, conditions and any other material information. Bookers must review the listing and confirm that it meets their requirements before paying.

1.4 No harassment, discrimination, fraud or unsafe conduct

Threats, stalking, unwanted contact, sexual harassment, inappropriate comments or touching, intimidation, discrimination, violence, abusive language, fraudulent listings, fake bookings, fare manipulation, coercion, illegal activity and misuse of another person's personal information are strictly prohibited. This applies to conduct in the app, during calls or messages arranged through Tripride, at pickup points and throughout a journey.

1.5 Agreed fare; no unauthorised extra money

A Publisher must not demand additional money after a booking is confirmed unless the additional charge was clearly disclosed in the listing and accepted by the Booker before confirmation, or is required due to a mutually agreed change. A Booker must not pressure a Publisher to provide unpaid extra services. Toll, parking, waiting, luggage or route-change charges must be disclosed and agreed in advance wherever practicable.

1.6 User-to-user interactions

Users must communicate respectfully and only for purposes connected with the relevant service. Do not share another user's phone number, identity document, live location, photographs or messages publicly without lawful authority or consent. Do not use contact details obtained through Tripride for spam, marketing, harassment or unrelated purposes.

1.7 Suspension and removal

Tripride may remove listings, restrict features, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant records and cooperate with lawful authorities where it reasonably suspects fraud, safety risks, harassment, unlawful activity, policy violations or misuse of the platform. Where appropriate and lawful, Tripride may seek the user's explanation before taking action. Users may report an action through the grievance contact below.

1.8 Third-party services and availability

Maps, location suggestions, OTP, payment processing, messaging, identity verification and other features may rely on third-party providers. Their services may be unavailable or subject to their own terms. Tripride does not guarantee uninterrupted availability, exact map accuracy, a match, a ride, a vehicle condition or a particular journey outcome.

1.9 Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Tripride is not responsible for independent user conduct, a Publisher's vehicle maintenance, driving decisions, permits, insurance, route choices, personal disputes, or promises made outside the platform. Tripride is not an insurer of a ride or vehicle. Nothing in these Terms excludes liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, breach of a non-excludable statutory duty, or any liability that cannot legally be limited.

1.10 Changes and governing law

Tripride may update these Terms to reflect product, operational or legal changes. The updated version will be posted with a revised date. These Terms are governed by the laws of India. Courts and competent authorities having jurisdiction under applicable law shall have jurisdiction; nothing here removes a consumer's statutory rights or access to a competent forum.

2. Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Tripride may collect, why it is used, when it is shared and how users can contact us about their information.

2.1 Information we may collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address, profile photo and account identifiers.
  • Ride and booking details: pickup location, destination, travel date/time, meeting point, seat count, booking history, ride code and support messages.
  • Location: current location, pickup and destination coordinates, and approximate route information when you grant device/browser permission or enter these details.
  • Publisher and vehicle details: vehicle type, make/model, registration number, photographs, seating capacity, insurance/permit/fitness information where required, and driving licence verification status.
  • Verification information: identity and age verification details. If Aadhaar or PAN verification is offered or required for a particular lawful purpose, we will request only information reasonably necessary for that purpose and provide an appropriate notice. We aim to use a verification provider or masked/tokenised result where feasible instead of retaining full identity document numbers or copies.
  • Payment information: payment status, transaction reference, amount, refund status and payment gateway response. Card, UPI PIN, bank login and other sensitive payment credentials are handled by the payment provider; Tripride should not request your UPI PIN, OTP or card PIN.
  • Technical information: device/browser information, IP address, log data, crash reports, cookies or similar technologies where used.

2.2 Why we use information

  • To create and secure accounts, authenticate users by mobile OTP and identify the relevant account.
  • To show publisher contact or vehicle information to a user who has made a relevant booking, where necessary to coordinate the journey.
  • To use current location, pickup and destination information for search, route display, nearby rides and navigation-related features.
  • To verify publishers, drivers, vehicles and users where appropriate for safety, fraud prevention or legal compliance.
  • To process bookings, collect disclosed platform fees, verify payments, issue refunds and maintain transaction records.
  • To provide support, handle complaints, investigate safety incidents, prevent misuse and comply with lawful requests.
  • To improve platform performance, security and user experience, and send service-related notices. Marketing communications will be handled in accordance with applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

2.3 When information may be shared

Relevant booking details may be shared with the Publisher and Booker so they can coordinate the trip. Payment-related information is shared with Razorpay or the payment provider to process payments and refunds. Location and map queries may be processed by Google Maps/Places or another map provider. OTP/authentication and database services may be provided by Firebase/Google or other vendors used by Tripride. Information may also be shared with verification providers, professional advisers, insurers where relevant, or law-enforcement/government authorities when legally required or necessary to protect users and rights.

We do not authorise users or service providers to use personal information for unrelated marketing, harassment, sale of contact lists or other unauthorised purposes.

2.4 Aadhaar, PAN and identity verification

Do not send Aadhaar/PAN documents through ordinary chat, email or public listing fields. If a verification flow is enabled, it must explain the exact purpose, fields collected, provider, retention period and user choices before collection. Tripride should not collect or store full Aadhaar details unless there is a lawful basis and a compliant process. PAN should likewise be collected only where necessary for a stated lawful purpose. Verification does not mean that Tripride guarantees a person's future conduct, driving ability or honesty.

2.5 Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, legal obligations, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and accounting. Retention periods depend on the type of record and applicable law. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, access controls and service-provider controls. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, Tripride will take steps required by applicable law, including communications to affected users or authorities where required.

2.6 Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a privacy complaint by contacting admin@tripride.co.in. Some records may need to be retained to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or complete transactions. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

2.7 Children and policy updates

Tripride is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal data without the legally required parent/guardian consent and safeguards. We may update this policy and will post the revised version and date on this page.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

This policy applies to bookings made through Tripride. The exact cancellation terms shown on the relevant service/checkout screen at the time of booking form part of the booking.

3.1 Passenger / Booker cancels a confirmed ride

As the requested business rule, if a Booker voluntarily cancels a ride after the booking has been confirmed, the booking amount is generally non-refundable. This rule must be displayed clearly before payment and confirmation. It will not apply where a refund is required by applicable law, where Tripride or the Publisher cancels, where the service is not provided as promised, or where Tripride approves a refund after reviewing exceptional circumstances.

3.2 Publisher cancels a confirmed ride

If the Publisher cancels a confirmed ride, or is unable to provide the booked service, the Booker will be eligible for a refund of the amount paid for that cancelled service, subject to transaction verification and applicable law. Any platform or convenience fee treatment must be shown clearly at checkout; where the cancellation is attributable to the Publisher or platform, Tripride should not retain a fee in a manner prohibited by law.

3.3 Other situations

  • Payment deducted but booking not confirmed: Tripride will verify the transaction and initiate a reversal/refund if no valid booking was created.
  • Duplicate payment: verified duplicate payments will be refunded.
  • Tripride cancels or removes a ride: eligible amounts for the affected booking will be refunded.
  • Publisher or Booker no-show: users should report the issue promptly with booking details and available evidence. Refund eligibility will be assessed under the displayed booking terms and applicable law.
  • Material route, date, vehicle or fare change: if the change is not accepted by the Booker, the Booker may request cancellation and a refund as required by the displayed terms and applicable law.
  • Partially completed journey or dispute: Tripride may review available booking records and communications; any refund will depend on the facts, the service terms and applicable law.

3.4 How to request a refund

Email admin@tripride.co.in or call 9951994651. Provide your registered mobile number, booking/ride ID, transaction reference, cancellation reason and supporting information. Do not send your UPI PIN, card PIN, password or OTP.

3.5 Processing time

Once approved, Tripride will initiate the refund through the original payment method or payment provider's supported process. The time taken for the amount to appear in the bank account/UPI/card depends on the bank, issuer, UPI network and payment provider. Tripride will share the refund reference/transaction status where available. A refund marked “initiated” may take additional time to be credited.

3.6 Refund exclusions and statutory rights

No clause in this policy removes rights that cannot legally be waived. Any non-refundable fee, gateway charge or cancellation charge must be disclosed before the user pays and must be lawful. Tripride may request reasonable evidence to prevent fraudulent refund claims.

4. Driver / Ride Creator / Publisher Policy

  • Publishers must be legally eligible to drive or provide the listed service and must hold a valid driving licence, registration, insurance, permit, fitness certificate and other documents required for that vehicle/service and jurisdiction.
  • Provide truthful identity, contact, vehicle registration, vehicle type, photos, seat availability, route, pickup/drop, date/time and fare information. Do not publish a ride using another person's identity or vehicle without authority.
  • Keep the vehicle roadworthy, clean and safe. Do not drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs or impairing medication. Follow traffic laws, speed limits, seat-belt/helmet requirements and passenger capacity limits.
  • Do not demand extra money, change the agreed fare, add passengers beyond permitted capacity or change the route materially without informing and obtaining agreement from the Booker, except where an emergency or legal requirement makes this unavoidable.
  • Do not cancel confirmed rides repeatedly, accept bookings you cannot honour, create fake rides, collect payment outside the disclosed flow to deceive users, or mark a ride complete when it did not occur.
  • Treat every passenger respectfully. Sexual harassment, unwanted touching, comments, intimidation, stalking, discrimination, threats or retaliation are prohibited. Do not ask a passenger for personal or intimate information unrelated to the trip.
  • Use passenger contact details and location only to coordinate the booked service. Do not retain, forward or use those details for unrelated contact after the trip.
  • Publishers must report accidents, safety incidents, vehicle changes and inability to complete a ride promptly. Cooperate with reasonable complaint and safety investigations.
  • Publishers are responsible for their own tax, permits, insurance, employment and regulatory obligations, as applicable. Tripride does not promise that a listing is a substitute for any licence or permit.

Verification badge or document check is not a guarantee of a Publisher's character, conduct, vehicle condition, driving skill or future safety.

5. Passenger / Booking Policy

  • Use your own accurate account details and make bookings only for yourself or people you are authorised to book for.
  • Review the route, pickup point, destination, date/time, vehicle information, fare, cancellation conditions and any special requirements before confirming.
  • Arrive at the agreed pickup point on time and keep your phone reachable. If delayed, contact the Publisher as early as possible.
  • Pay only the fare and disclosed charges agreed through the Tripride booking flow or directly agreed in advance with the Publisher where the service expressly permits it. Do not agree to hidden or unsafe charges; report demands for unauthorised extra money.
  • Do not harass, threaten, abuse, discriminate against, touch or intimidate the Publisher or other passengers. Do not damage the vehicle, carry prohibited/illegal items or exceed agreed luggage limits.
  • Wear seat belts or helmets as required, follow safety instructions and do not pressure the driver to speed, break traffic laws or carry excess passengers.
  • Do not make fake bookings, repeatedly reserve seats without intent to travel, misuse refund requests or falsely report a Publisher.
  • Use the Publisher's contact details only for the confirmed booking. Do not publish or share their number, documents, vehicle details or location without lawful authority or consent.
  • Report a no-show, fare dispute, unsafe conduct, harassment or accident promptly through Tripride support and, where necessary, the police/emergency services.

6. Safety Policy

6.1 Before the journey

  • Check the vehicle registration and vehicle description against the booking details before entering the vehicle.
  • Confirm the Publisher's identity using the information shown in the app and share trip details with a trusted contact if you wish.
  • Do not board if the vehicle, driver, route or circumstances appear materially different or unsafe. Report the issue.
  • Publishers must confirm the passenger/booking details without demanding unnecessary identity information.

6.2 During the journey

  • Use seat belts/helmets and follow traffic laws. Do not distract the driver or request unsafe driving.
  • Users must not engage in harassment, sexual misconduct, threats, violence, discrimination or unwanted physical contact.
  • Use the in-app SOS feature if available. If there is immediate danger, call India's emergency number 112 or contact local police/emergency services directly.

6.3 SOS and nearby emergency places

Where enabled, Tripride may provide an SOS shortcut and map-based discovery of nearby police stations and hospitals using available location and map services. Nearby-place results may be incomplete, outdated or inaccurate and are not an emergency dispatch service. Users should independently call 112 or the relevant emergency provider and follow official instructions. Do not delay emergency action while using the app.

6.4 Incident reporting

Report accidents, threats, harassment, unsafe driving, suspected fraud or other serious incidents to admin@tripride.co.in or 9951994651. In an emergency, contact emergency services first. Tripride may preserve relevant platform records and cooperate with lawful authorities. Tripride cannot guarantee emergency response, police action, hospital availability or the outcome of an investigation.

6.5 Verification limitations

Any identity, phone, licence, PAN, Aadhaar or vehicle verification is limited to the scope and time of the check and available source/provider data. Verification does not guarantee that a user will behave safely or lawfully.

7. Payment Policy

7.1 Payment provider

Tripride uses Razorpay or another payment provider enabled at checkout to process supported online payments. Payment processing is subject to the provider's terms, security controls and availability. Tripride may receive transaction status, payment reference, amount and refund status; sensitive payment credentials should remain with the payment provider.

7.2 Platform/service fees

Any platform, convenience, booking, listing or service fee will be shown to the user before payment. Based on the current pricing information supplied for this draft, the intended platform/service fee range is ₹1 to ₹50, with a possible ₹10–₹20 platform fee for certain bike/car categories. These amounts are not final until the actual checkout pricing is configured and displayed. The amount charged at checkout is the applicable amount for that transaction.

Any ₹1,000 / ₹2,000 bike or car amount mentioned in a listing must be clearly labelled as the relevant ride, rental, package or vehicle price—not as a Tripride platform fee—if that is what the amount represents. Do not publish ambiguous or conflicting fee information.

7.3 Fare and other charges

The Publisher's fare or cost contribution is separate from Tripride's platform fee. Toll, parking, waiting, extra luggage, route change, taxes and other charges must be disclosed and agreed in advance where applicable. A Publisher must not demand an undisclosed extra amount after confirmation.

7.4 Failed, pending or duplicate transactions

If money is debited but the booking status is pending or failed, contact support with the transaction reference. Tripride will check the payment provider's status and arrange a refund/reversal where appropriate. Do not make repeated payments until you have checked the booking and transaction status.

7.5 Payment safety

Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs, passwords or banking credentials with a Publisher, another user or anyone claiming to be Tripride support. Tripride support will not ask for your UPI PIN or card PIN to issue a refund.
