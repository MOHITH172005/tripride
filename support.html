Frequently asked

Quick answers

When will the Tripride app launch?

Tripride is in development. Store links will be added as soon as the app is ready.

Are the listings on this website real?

No. Trips, rides, packages, vehicles and commuters shown here are sample data for design preview.

Do payments or bookings work yet?

Not yet. Booking and payments will be introduced with the platform launch.

How does cost sharing work?

Travellers agree the shared cost among themselves before a trip or ride.

What is Rydora?

Rydora connects people who commute the same route every day so they can share morning and evening rides.

What is Place to Place?

Place to Place is intercity ride sharing. You can share a ride between cities and split the agreed cost with fellow travellers.

What are Tour Packages?

Tour Packages are multi-destination journeys with complete itineraries, made for groups, families and couples.

Can I list my vehicle?

You can try a draft listing on the Buy / Sell page. Publishing will be enabled at launch.

How can I contact Tripride?

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 99519 94651, email admin@tripride.co.in, or use the message form on this page.

How can I share feedback or ask about a partnership?

Use the message form and choose Feedback or Partnership as the topic, or write to admin@tripride.co.in. We read every message.
