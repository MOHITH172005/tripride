Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This policy explains what information TripRide collects, why it's collected, and how it's used across the TripRide app and this website.

1. Information we collect

To provide trip discovery, ride sharing, bookings and chat, TripRide collects:

  • Account information — name, phone number, email address and profile photo when you sign up.
  • Trip and booking data — routes, dates, seats, passenger names and ages you enter when creating or booking a trip.
  • Payment information — transaction and payment-status data processed through our payment partner. TripRide does not store your card, UPI PIN or full payment credentials.
  • Location data — approximate or precise location, only when a location-aware feature is in use and you've granted permission.
  • Messages — content you send through in-app chat with other users, to deliver and display the conversation.
  • Device and usage data — device type, app version and crash/performance logs used to keep the app reliable.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate core features: trip discovery, bookings, chat, notifications and profile/QR sharing.
  • To process payments and verify payment status for bookings and agency registrations.
  • To keep the app secure, restore your session, and troubleshoot errors.
  • To send app notifications relevant to your trips and account.

3. How your information is stored

TripRide uses Firebase-backed cloud infrastructure to store account, trip and chat data, and a Razorpay-backed payment backend to process transactions. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

4. Sharing of information

  • Trip creators and passengers can see the profile and passenger details relevant to a shared trip.
  • Payment processors receive the information required to complete and verify a transaction.
  • TripRide does not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Your choices

  • You can edit your profile information from within the app.
  • You can control location and notification permissions from your device settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data — see our Delete Account page.

6. Data retention

We retain account and trip data for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period afterward where needed to resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or maintain payment records.

7. Children's privacy

TripRide is not directed at children, and the service is intended for users who meet the minimum age required to enter a booking agreement in their jurisdiction.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TripRide's features evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Questions about your data?
