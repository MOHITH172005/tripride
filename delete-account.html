Delete your TripRide account

Last updated: 22 August 2026

You can permanently delete your TripRide account and associated data. This page explains what gets removed and how to request it.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, photo, phone number and email tied to your account.
  • Saved trips, trip history and chat conversations linked to your account.
  • Your session and login credentials.

What may be retained

  • Payment and booking records required for accounting, dispute-handling or legal/tax obligations, retained for the minimum period required by law.
  • Information a trip creator or passenger already has as part of a completed booking with you is not retroactively removed from their own records.

How to request deletion

1
From the appGo to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, and confirm the request.
2
Or by emailSend a deletion request from your registered email or phone number using the form below or by emailing support directly.
3
ConfirmationWe verify the request and confirm once your account and associated data have been deleted, typically within a few business days.
Account deletion is permanent. Any active trip bookings should be completed or cancelled first — deleting your account does not automatically cancel a confirmed booking with another user.
