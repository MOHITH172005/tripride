TripRide Account Deletion Request

At TripRide, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. You may request deletion of your TripRide account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the TripRide app.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Select "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm your deletion request.

If you are unable to access the app, you may send an account deletion request to:

Email: valasareddymohith17@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Trip history associated with your account
  • Saved preferences and account settings

Data Retention

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required by law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security, or regulatory compliance.

Deletion Timeline

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification.
