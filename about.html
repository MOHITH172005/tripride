About Us

Built for people who love to move together

Tripride is a travel-tech startup making shared journeys simple, social and affordable.

Our servicesContact us
Our Story

Travel is better together.

Tripride began with a simple observation: people often travel the same routes alone, with empty seats and higher costs, while others look for company or a ride.

We are building a travel-tech platform that connects them. From weekend trips and intercity journeys to daily office commutes, Tripride helps people share the way.

What we stand for

Responsible travel

Our Mission

Travel beyond ordinary.

  • Make travel more affordable and social.
  • Help people discover companions and routes.
  • Bring daily commuters together with Rydora.
  • Give every journey, from local to long-distance, one trusted home.
